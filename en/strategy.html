The strategy

A complete method, with clear rules.

Centinela starts from a simple idea: no asset works well all the time. That is why we work with blocks that respond differently to each economic regime and, on that foundation, apply momentum signals, trend filters and disciplined rebalancing with explicit criteria.

Diversification across cycles

Four regimes, one strategy.

Each block responds to a different economic environment. The logic starts from a simple idea, inspired by Artemis Capital’s Dragon Portfolio: build a strategy better able to adapt to the different stages of the cycle.

Growth

Sustained expansion with contained inflation. Exposure is tilted towards global equities.

Recession / deflation

Credit contraction and flight to quality. Treasuries and TIPS act as a safe haven and cushion falls in risk assets.

Inflation / debasement

Loss of the currency’s purchasing power. Gold, silver, copper and commodities help preserve real value.

Extreme volatility

Crises, severe corrections and tail events. Long-vol hedges add extra protection in stress scenarios.

The method

Four pillars that shape the strategy.

  1. 1
    Diversification across cycles. Five permanent blocks that respond to different economic regimes, so the strategy does not depend on a single scenario.
  2. 2
    Relative momentum selection. Within each block we select the three assets with the greatest relative strength, measured over a 6-month window.
  3. 3
    Trend filter. If an asset loses its trend, it is temporarily replaced with short-term Treasuries (SHY) until it regains strength. This is how we reduce exposure when conditions deteriorate.
  4. 4
    Monthly rebalancing. The strategy is reviewed with explicit rules and automatic alerts. A systematic, disciplined process with no improvised decisions.
