About Centinela

Momentum, discipline, independence and integrity.

Centinela was born from the idea of understanding the market better in order to make investment decisions with sounder judgement. We believe in a clear, systematic and transparent way of investing, based on rules rather than hunches or impulses.

We don’t want to complicate investing with empty promises or hard-to-follow language. We want to offer an understandable methodology, a structured reading of the market and a consistent way of acting over time.

Our values

Four principles that guide every decision.

  • MomentumFollow the market’s strength, not opinions or the headline of the day.
  • DisciplineExplicit rules, no exceptions. Method over impulse.
  • IndependenceNo incentive to push our own products and no conflicts of interest.
  • IntegrityFull transparency in every decision and every result.
Who it’s for

Is Centinela right for you?

Centinela is designed for people with an intermediate knowledge of markets, a stable income and the ability to save, who want to invest with sounder judgement and follow a clear methodology.

See if it’s right for you
  • You have a portfolio and want to give it more method.
  • You save regularly and think long term.
  • You prefer clear rules to impulsive decisions.
  • You want to understand what you do and why.
