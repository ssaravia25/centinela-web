The problem

Most people invest with less clarity than they think.

Many investors end up in products with high fees, poorly structured decisions or strategies they never fully understand. With so much on offer, so much noise and so little time, investing with sound judgement becomes harder than it should be.

  • 01

    Expensive funds

    Actively managed products that rarely beat the market over the long term, yet keep high cost structures.

  • 02

    Too much noise

    Too much information, too many options and too little context to decide calmly and sensibly.

  • 03

    No time, no method

    People with a genuine interest in investing better, but without the time or technical depth to build a solid routine on their own.

How it works

Designed for different economic environments.

No asset works well all the time. That is why the portfolio combines blocks that respond differently to growth, recession, inflation and episodes of extreme volatility. On that foundation we apply momentum signals, trend filters and disciplined rebalancing, with clear rules and continuous monitoring.

Growth

Sustained expansion with contained inflation. Captured through global equities.

See how it behaves

Global equities lead when the economy grows.

Recession / deflation

Credit contraction and flight to quality. Bonds act as a safe haven.

See how it behaves

Bonds act as a safe haven while the stock market falls.

Inflation / debasement

Paper money erodes. Gold and commodities preserve real value.

See how it behaves

Gold and commodities preserve value while cash erodes.

Extreme volatility

Crises and black swans. Hedges work like insurance in extreme events.

See how it behaves

Hedges soar just as the stock market plunges: they act as insurance.

The four pillars of the method

  1. 1
    Diversification across cycles. Permanent blocks covering every economic regime.
  2. 2
    Momentum selection. Within each block, the assets with the greatest relative strength.
  3. 3
    Trend filter. If an asset deteriorates, risk is reduced until it regains strength.
  4. 4
    Monthly rebalancing. A review with explicit rules and automatic alerts.
